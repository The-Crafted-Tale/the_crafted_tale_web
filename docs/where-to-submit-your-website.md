# Where to Submit Your Website

A guide for **The Crafted Tale** (and similar sites) on where to register your site so people can find you via search, analytics, and social channels.

## Search engine submissions (essential)

- **[Google Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console)** — The most important one. You verify ownership of your domain, submit your sitemap, and monitor how Google crawls and indexes your site. It also shows which keywords people use to find you, click-through rates, and any crawl errors.

- **[Bing Webmaster Tools](https://www.bing.com/webmasters)** — Similar to Google but for Bing (and it also feeds Yahoo, DuckDuckGo, and others in some cases). Verify your site and submit your sitemap here too.

## Analytics (you already have Vercel Analytics, but consider)

- **[Google Analytics](https://analytics.google.com)** — More detailed visitor analytics than Vercel’s built-in. Helps you understand user behavior, traffic sources, and conversions. Free.

## Business listings (important for a product / gift business)

- **[Google Business Profile](https://business.google.com)** — If you have a physical location or serve a local area, this is critical. Even for online-only brands, it can help with credibility.

- **[Pinterest Business](https://business.pinterest.com)** — Very relevant for crafted and gift products. Pinterest drives a lot of traffic for visual product categories.

- **Instagram / Facebook** — Create business pages and link to your website. Social signals can indirectly support SEO.

## Directories and marketplaces (optional, good for backlinks)

- **Etsy** or **Amazon Handmade** — If it fits your model, list products there and link back to your main site.

- **Yelp**, **Yellow Pages** — For local business presence.

- **Product Hunt** — If you want exposure in the tech / startup community.
