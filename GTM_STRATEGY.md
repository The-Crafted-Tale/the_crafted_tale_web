# Go-To-Market Strategy — The Crafted Tale
### Minimal-Cost Playbook for a Handmade Gifts Studio

---

## TL;DR

Focus 80% of effort on **Instagram + WhatsApp + SEO**. These three channels cost nothing but time and have the highest conversion rate for a handmade, trust-driven product. Layer in Google Business Profile and Pinterest for passive discovery. Add paid only after organic proof-of-concept.

---

## 1. Organic Social — Instagram (Priority #1)

**Why:** Handmade gifts are inherently visual. Instagram's discovery algorithms heavily favour craft and aesthetic content. Your target buyers (people gifting for weddings, birthdays, festivals) scroll Instagram daily.

**What to post:**
- **Process Reels (60%)** — time-lapses and close-ups of hands making a product. "Making of" content consistently outperforms product shots.
- **Before/After (20%)** — raw materials → finished gift. Tap into the satisfaction of transformation.
- **Customer stories (20%)** — reshare tagged photos, DM customers for testimonial reels with their permission.

**Tactics:**
- Post 4–5x per week (Reels > Stories > Carousels in reach priority).
- Use 8–12 niche hashtags per post: mix `#handmadegifts` (large), `#customgiftsIndia` (medium), `#personalizedgifts[City]` (local, low-competition).
- Pin your 3 best-performing Reels to your profile grid.
- Add a clear CTA in every caption: "DM to order" or "Link in bio."
- Engage back within the first 30 minutes of posting — comments in that window boost algorithmic reach significantly.

**Cost:** ₹0 (time only)

---

## 2. WhatsApp Business — Conversion Engine (Priority #2)

**Why:** Your existing site already drives WhatsApp inquiries. WhatsApp closes faster than email because it's personal and synchronous.

**Setup (free):**
- Set up **WhatsApp Business** app (free): business profile with hours, catalogue, and auto-replies.
- Create a **Catalogue** inside WhatsApp Business — mirror your product listings with prices. Customers can share products directly from WhatsApp.
- Add a **Quick Reply** for the most common question: "How long does delivery take?"
- Use **Labels** to tag leads: "New Inquiry", "Price Shared", "Order Confirmed", "Pending Payment".

**Broadcast Lists (₹0):**
- Build a list of past customers (opt-in only).
- Send 2–3 broadcasts per month around festivals: "Diwali specials are here — reply to see the lookbook."
- Keep broadcasts personal in tone — never mass-markety.

**Cost:** ₹0 (WhatsApp Business is free for individuals/small businesses)

---

## 3. Google Business Profile — Local SEO (Priority #3)

**Why:** When someone Googles "handmade gifts [city]" or "personalised gift shop near me", GBP is what surfaces in the map pack — above organic results. Completely free.

**Setup:**
1. Claim your profile at `business.google.com`.
2. Select category: **Gift Shop** + **Handmade Crafts**.
3. Add all photos: products, packaging, process shots (aim for 20+).
4. Write a keyword-rich description: include "custom handmade gifts", "personalised gifts India", your city name.
5. Enable **Messaging** so customers can contact you directly from search.
6. Add **Products** to the GBP listing (these appear in search results).

**Ongoing (10 min/week):**
- Post 1 GBP update per week (new product, seasonal offer).
- Reply to every review within 24 hours — Google rewards responsiveness with ranking boosts.
- Actively request reviews from happy customers via WhatsApp: share the direct review link.

**Cost:** ₹0

---

## 4. SEO — Long-Term Organic Traffic

Your site already has strong technical SEO foundations (schema markup, sitemap, canonical URLs, ISR). The gap is **content**.

**Quick wins (no-code):**
- Make sure every product has a unique, descriptive name and a 2–3 sentence description with natural keywords (e.g., "hand-folded paper gift box for weddings", not just "gift box").
- Add alt text to all product images that describes the item + occasion: "handmade personalized birthday gift box with ribbon, India."

**Content that earns rankings over time (1 post/month):**
Create a simple blog (Nuxt Content or a free Notion-embedded page works) targeting long-tail questions:
- "What to gift on a best friend's wedding in India" → link to your custom product.
- "Personalised birthday gift ideas under ₹500" → link to your ready-made range.
- "How to order a custom hamper for Diwali" → explains your order process.

These posts rank on Google and bring people already in buying intent.

**Cost:** ₹0 (time) + ₹0–₹500/mo for a domain blog if needed

## 5. Pinterest — Passive Discovery Engine

**Why:** Pinterest is a search engine for gift inspiration. Content lives for months, unlike Instagram posts. Indian gifting searches spike on Pinterest around Diwali, Valentine's Day, Raksha Bandhan.

**Setup:**
- Create a **Pinterest Business** account (free).
- Create boards: "Handmade Wedding Gifts", "Custom Birthday Gifts India", "Personalised Gift Boxes", "Diwali Gift Ideas."
- Pin every product photo with keyword-rich descriptions and a link back to the product page.
- Repin 5–10 relevant pins from others per week to boost account visibility.

**Cost:** ₹0

---

## 6. Micro-Influencer Collaborations (Low Cost)

**Why:** Macro-influencers are expensive and have low trust. Micro-influencers (5K–50K followers in lifestyle, wedding, or mom niches) have 3–5× higher engagement and their audiences trust them.

**How to do it cheaply:**
- Identify 5–10 local lifestyle/wedding/gifting creators on Instagram.
- Offer a **product exchange** (no cash): send them one of your products, ask for an honest Reel or Story with a tag if they love it.
- Look specifically for creators who make content around: "gift ideas", "wedding planning India", "unboxing", "sustainable lifestyle."
- Target creators in metros first (Mumbai, Delhi, Bangalore, Hyderabad) — higher gifting spend.

**Cost:** ₹300–₹800 per product (materials + shipping only)

## 7. Festive Calendar — Peak Demand Windows

Plan your inventory and content calendar around India's gifting peaks:

| Festival / Occasion | Months | Lead Time Needed |
|---|---|---|
| Valentine's Day | February | Start Jan 15 |
| Holi | March | Start Feb 20 |
| Mother's Day | May | Start Apr 25 |
| Raksha Bandhan | Aug | Start Jul 20 |
| Diwali | Oct–Nov | **Biggest window** — Start Sep 1 |
| Christmas / New Year | December | Start Nov 20 |
| Wedding Season | Nov–Feb | Run evergreen campaigns |

**For each window:**
- Post a "Gifting Guide" Reel 3 weeks before.
- Add a limited-time message on your site (a simple banner or CTA copy update).
- Broadcast via WhatsApp to past buyers 2 weeks before.

---

## 8. Referral Loop — Word of Mouth

**Why:** For trust-based, handmade products, a recommendation from a friend converts better than any ad.

**Simple referral mechanics (no app needed):**
- Include a handwritten note in every order: "Loved your gift? Share us with a friend and they get ₹50 off their first order — just have them mention your name."
- After delivery, WhatsApp the customer: "Hope you loved it! If you'd like to share us with a friend, here's a direct link to our shop 🙏" — one line, no pressure.
- Track referrals manually via a simple Google Sheet until volume justifies a tool.

**Cost:** ₹0 (note paper + time)

## Channels to Skip (For Now)

| Channel | Why to Skip |
|---|---|
| Facebook Ads | CPM rising, Indian handmade niche saturated |
| Google Ads | Low ROI for brand-new sites with no conversion history |
| Etsy | Good long-term, but 6.5% transaction fee + listing fees eat margins |
| Email newsletters | Premature — build audience first |
| LinkedIn | Wrong audience for gifting products |

Add paid advertising only after you have:
1. ≥ 50 organic orders (proof the product converts)
2. A clear best-selling product to run ads to
3. A landing page with a strong offer and testimonials
